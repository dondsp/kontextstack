# Provider and approval procedure

Inspect the selected production surfaces and their existing owner procedures without collecting secrets or real customer records. Show the exact release revision, audience, evidence gaps and recovery references for owner review. Obtain separate approval before hosted acceptance mutations, backup/export, restore, credential rotation or incident containment. A read-only check is not approval to restart, alter DNS or change data. Scope one failing layer at a time and preserve non-secret observations. Confirm support window, incident severity, escalation/communication owner and observation period. After approved recovery repeat health, representative journey, persistence and authorization where selected; distinguish expected data loss, recovery point and recovery time. Ask the product owner to accept or reject the release only after the required evidence passes.

Every action needs exact target, effect, owner, explicit approval, expected result, verification and recovery. Actual provider labels must be inspected. Paste non-secret evidence only; remove credentials, environment values, private keys, customer records and production rows. Review screenshots before retention.
